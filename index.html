Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how [Developer/Company Name] ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use the REMbank mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect:

The App is designed to store your data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate servers to collect or store your personal sleep data remotely. The information handled by the App includes:

  • Sleep Data: Information you manually enter about your sleep sessions, including start/end times, calculated hours, target hours, wake count, bedtime, wake time, nap times, and any notes or tags you associate with entries.
  • Journal Entries: Text entries you create during the wind-down activity.
  • Sleep Credits & Challenges: Calculated sleep credits and logs of completed wind-down or challenge activities.
  • Preferences: Settings you configure within the App, such as sleep target, reminder settings, smart alarm preferences, and desired sleep schedule.
  • HealthKit Data (Optional): If you choose to connect the App to Apple HealthKit, we request permission to read sleep analysis data. This data is processed on your device to create corresponding sleep entries within the App and calculate associated credits. We do not store raw HealthKit data persistently outside of the standard HealthKit store managed by iOS, nor do we transmit it off your device. Access to HealthKit data requires your explicit permission via the standard iOS HealthKit prompts.
  • Apple Sign In Data (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier, and potentially your name and email address (depending on the permissions you grant during the sign-in process). This information is stored locally on your device to manage your account status within the App.
  • Purchase Information (Handled by Apple): If you subscribe to REMbank Premium or purchase the lifetime option, the purchase process is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We receive verification from Apple about your purchase status (e.g., whether you are a premium subscriber) but do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Analytics (Usage Data): The App may collect anonymized, aggregated usage data strictly for the purpose of identifying crashes, improving app performance, and understanding feature usage. This data does not personally identify you. (Example: We might track how many users use the Smart Alarm feature, but not who uses it or their specific settings).

How We Use Your Information:

  • To provide the App's core functionality (tracking sleep, calculating debt/score/credits, providing stats, enabling features like Smart Alarm and Planner).
  • To store your data locally on your device.
  • To process HealthKit data (if permission is granted) for integration into the App.
  • To manage your premium subscription status based on verification from Apple.
  • To analyze anonymized usage data for improving the App.

Data Storage and Security:

All your personal sleep data, journal entries, preferences, and linked Apple Sign In identifiers are stored locally on your device within the App's secure container using SwiftData. We do not transmit this personal data to external servers.

While we strive to protect your information, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Data stored locally is subject to the security of your device itself.

Data Sharing:

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Anonymized, aggregated usage data may be used for internal analysis or shared with service providers for analytics purposes (e.g., Apple App Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics - please specify if used).

Your Control and Data Deletion:

You can view and manage your data within the App. You have the option within the App's settings to delete all your recorded sleep entries, credits, and journal entries. Deleting the App from your device will also remove the locally stored data.

You can manage HealthKit permissions at any time in the Apple Health app settings on your device.

You can manage App Store subscriptions through your Apple ID account settings on your device.

Children's Privacy:

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
